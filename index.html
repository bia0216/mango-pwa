      Good to know

      • Made for the long yellow mango, one mango per photo.
      • Photos that don't look like a mango should be answered with "Not a mango". Look-alike fruit can still pass.
      • Disease check: Anthracnose, Stem-end rot and Black spot (bacterial canker). See the Disease tab after a scan.
      • Prediction runs on the project's model server, so an internet connection is needed.
