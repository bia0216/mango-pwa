Good to know

  • Made for the long yellow mango, one mango per photo.
  • The model always picks one of three classes (unripe, ripe, overripe). It cannot detect disease or rot, and it does not reject non-mango photos.
  • Prediction runs on the project's model server, so an internet connection is needed.
Data: Roboflow “mango-fruit-ripeness” (school-vakkx, CC BY 4.0) and “dataset-s2doa” (emm-tnn, Public Domain).
